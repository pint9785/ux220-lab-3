The Best Movie I’ve Watched Recently

By Carolina Pinto

Before watching the movie, I did not know what the movie was about but I think that was the best way to watch it as the movie takes viewers on a rollercoaster of a watching experience, while still being realistic and clever. I would also recommend watching as a Canadian as many Canadians can watch the film and see many popular spots that only true Canadians and Toronto goers would know about. If not, the movie is still packed with silly jokes, clever references and great comedic timing. All around the movie was great and I have watched it twice now as it has become one of my favorite movies!

Recently I have watched the movie, ‘Nirvanna the Band the Show the Movie’. The film is a Canadian science fiction comedy that takes place in Toronto, following the wacky lives of these two friends trying to get their band to play at a local venue, which doesn’t seem like to crazy of a story but the film turns into a “back to the Future” type of film as they discover a time machine in their old RV.
